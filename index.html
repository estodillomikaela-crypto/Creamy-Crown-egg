Our Philosophy

"Once you try it, You'll Crave it!"

At Creamy Crown Egg, street food is elevated to a royal snack experience. We carefully prepare hard-boiled eggs with a double panko coating, flash-fry them to golden crispness, and pipe them with our secret whipped, velvety seasoned yolk cream. From garlic parmesan indulgence to spicy barbeque glaze, every bite satisfies your deepest cravings.

Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is a Creamy Crown Egg? +
It is a gourmet twist on crispy deviled eggs! We take hard-boiled eggs, give them an airy panko crunch shell, and fill them with silky whipped yolk blended with special seasoning.
How much does it cost? +
Our individual signature flavors are just ₱35 per cup! We also offer the Crown Trio Box (3 pcs) for ₱100 and Party Platters (5 pcs) for ₱170.

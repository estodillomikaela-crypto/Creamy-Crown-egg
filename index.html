Our Story

"Once you try it, You'll Crave it!"

At Creamy Crown Egg, we believe street food can be an extraordinary gourmet experience. We take tender hard-boiled eggs, coat them in crunchy Japanese panko, fry them golden, and crown them with seasoned, velvety whipped yolk filling and rich artisanal toppings. From comforting Classic to savory Garlic Parmesan, every bite is crafted to deliver royal satisfaction at street-friendly prices.

About Us

"Once You Try It, You'll Crave It!"

At Creamy Crown Egg, started with a simple idea to make the usual deviled egg more exciting. We wanted to create something that people would enjoy not only because of its taste, but also because of its crispy texture and unique presentation. We made a creamy and flavorful filling and paired it with a crispy, fried, and breaded coating. After trying out different ideas and working together as a team, we came up with a snack that is crunchy on the outside and creamy on the inside. Creamy Crown Egg is our way of turning a simple and familiar ingredient into something fun, delicious, and different. We wanted it to feel a little fancy while still being affordable, convenient, and enjoyable for everyone.

Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is a Creamy Crown Egg? +
It is a gourmet twist on crispy deviled eggs! We take hard-boiled eggs, give them an airy panko crunch shell, and fill them with silky whipped yolk blended with special seasoning and flavorful toppings.
What flavors are available? +
We currently offer five Royal Signature Flavors: Classic, Cheesy Bacon, Spinach Cheese, Garlic Parmesan, and Spicy Barbeque.
How much does it cost? +
All our signature flavors are just ₱35 per cup.
Can I pre-order for events or parties? +
Yes! We welcome bulk orders and event pre-orders at just ₱35 per cup. Simply call us at 09603794271 or message us through our social media channels.
Where are you located? +
You can find Creamy Crown Egg at F Legaspi St. Ugong, Pasig City. For orders, availability, and inquiries, please contact us at 09603794271.
What modes of payment do you accept? +
We accept Cash on Delivery (COD) only. Please prepare exact payment upon receiving your fresh order from our rider.

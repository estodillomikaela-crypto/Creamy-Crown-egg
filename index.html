About Us

"Once You Try It, You'll Crave It!"

At Creamy Crown Egg, street food is elevated to a royal snack experience. We carefully prepare hard-boiled eggs with a double panko coating, flash-fry them to golden crispness, and pipe them with our secret whipped, velvety seasoned yolk cream. From garlic parmesan indulgence to spicy barbecue glaze, every bite is crafted to satisfy your cravings.

Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is a Creamy Crown Egg? +
It is a gourmet twist on crispy deviled eggs! We take hard-boiled eggs, give them an airy panko crunch shell, and fill them with silky whipped yolk blended with special seasoning and flavorful toppings.
What flavors are available? +
We currently offer five Royal Signature Flavors: Classic, Cheesy Bacon, Spinach Cheese, Garlic Parmesan, and Spicy Barbeque.
How much does it cost? +
Our individual signature flavors are just ₱35 per cup. We also offer the Crown Trio Box (3 pcs) for ₱100 and Party Platters (5 pcs) for ₱170.
Can I pre-order for events or parties? +
Yes! We welcome party boxes and bulk orders. Simply call us at 09603794271 or message us through our social media channels.
Where are you located? +
You can find Creamy Crown Egg at F Legaspi St. Ugong, Pasig City. For orders, availability, and inquiries, please contact us at 09603794271.
